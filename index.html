Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Rewardly collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal details that you provide directly to us when creating or logging into your account. This includes:

  • Profile Information: Your full name, email address, and profile picture received from your Google Account during OAuth sign-in.
  • Device Metadata: Your device model name, operating system version, and unique device hardware identifier to prevent referral program abuse.
  • Push Tokens: Your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token to send instant transaction updates and notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, performance of our contract with you, and compliance with our legal obligations:

  • To deliver rewards, manage points, and process withdrawal requests.
  • To validate referral codes and prevent users from claiming multiple referrals on the same device.
  • To notify you about important updates, approved withdrawals, or rejected request reasons.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with third-party service providers (such as Google Sign-In and Firebase) to facilitate app operations. These providers are bound by privacy requirements and cannot use your data for unrelated purposes.

4. Security of Data

We implement security measures including HTTPS communication and controlled access mechanisms to help protect your information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed as completely secure.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You may sign out from Google login, manage notification permissions from your device settings, or contact us to request deletion of your account and associated information.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

support@rewardly.io
